# Signing in

Everyone signs in with their own username and password at `/login` ("Enter WhoDoo"). The page shows
the club as a tree by rank: President, Treasurer, Event leads, Members, Volunteers. Picking a person
opens a box for their username and password; the credentials must belong to the person picked.

- `make credentials` gives anyone without a login a username and a random password and writes the list
  to `credentials/` (`WhoDoo_credentials.xlsx`, `credentials.csv`). `make credentials-reset` gives
  everybody a new password. `make seed` does this at the end.
- `credentials/` is in `.gitignore`. It is the only readable copy of the passwords; the database keeps
  salted scrypt hashes only (`user_credentials`, migration 045).
- A wrong username and a wrong password give the same answer; five wrong tries lock a username for
  five minutes. People change their own password with `PUT /api/v1/auth/password`.
- The old passwordless picker (`/dev/login`) is removed. Ranks and what each may do are described in
  `app/services/auth_service.py`.

# Printing, emails and cash flow

- **Printing.** A ticket prints as one bordered sheet (portrait) and a certificate as one landscape
  sheet; the rest of the page is left out. Stylesheets and scripts carry a version in their address,
  so a browser picks up changes without a forced reload.
- **Emails.** Ticket and certificate emails share one layout in the site's colours. A certificate is
  emailed to its recipient when issued, and can be sent again from its page.
- **Cash flow.** `/treasurer/events/<id>` shows one event's money in (registrations, tickets), money
  out by category (paid, approved, waiting), its budget and every ledger entry. The finance dashboard
  shows money generated by source, merchandise, and in / out / net per event. Expense categories
  now include Prizes and Goodies. Everything is read from the ledger.

# Everything is on by default

Memberships, Tickets, Check-in, Announcements, Certificates, Handover and Member 2's additions are
ordinary migrations now (`migrations/020` to `043`), and FairSeat is on unless `FAIRSEAT_ENABLED=0`.
`make migrate` is all a database needs; `make seed` loads the demo data for every module. Sections
below that mention `make modules` or a "pending" folder describe how it used to work.

- **Front page.** An events calendar: a month grid, and a panel listing everything on the chosen
  day with a Register button. Published and completed events of every club; no drafts or cancelled.
- **Menu.** The three-bar menu lists every section, with the shop (Store, My orders) apart from
  running it (Merchandise: Merch admin, Merch orders), and Profile and Notifications under Account.
- **Crew clashes** (`/crew/review`). Standing duplicate tasks and overlapping duties across open
  events, and the times an organiser assigned anyway, with the reason. President and event leads.
- **Check-in.** The camera scanner works in every browser: the built-in detector where there is
  one, otherwise the ZXing library in `app/static/vendor/`. The guest list can be searched and
  filtered, and the count updates as people come in. Browsers only allow the camera on `https://`
  or `localhost`; elsewhere the page says so and offers the typed code and the guest list.
- **Certificates** print on one A4 landscape page.

# Public event registration (always on)

Any student can open `/events` without signing in, see upcoming events from every club, and
register. Signed in, `/events` is the club's own management page as before (the public list is
at `/explore`).

- **Setting an event up.** On the event form the lead picks free, paid or no online
  registration, the number of participants, and a venue. Only venues that hold that many
  people are offered (ten college venues are loaded by `migrations/012_public_events.sql`).
  An event cannot be published with a venue that is too small or a paid event without a price.
- **Registering.** Name, college student ID, email and phone. One place per student per event.
  The limit is enforced under a lock: when every place is taken the answer is "All N places
  for this event are taken".
- **Paid events** use the same payment provider as merchandise (Stripe test mode, or the mock).
  The place is held for 30 minutes while paying. A payment that arrives after the place went to
  someone else is refunded.
- **The ticket** is a QR code, shown on a private ticket page and emailed. Each code is an HMAC
  of the registration's own id, so no two are alike, and the database also refuses a duplicate.
  It admits once: the door page (`/check-in`) shows the name and student ID, and a second scan
  says when it was already used.
- **Email.** Set `SMTP_HOST=smtp.gmail.com`, `SMTP_USER` and `SMTP_PASSWORD` (a Gmail app
  password) in `.env` to send real emails. Without them, emails are written to `outbox/`.
- **Retention.** Participant details are deleted 60 days after the event ends
  (`REGISTRATION_RETENTION_DAYS`), about once a day by the app or with `make purge-registrations`.
- **Participant or crew, not both.** Someone registered to attend cannot be given tasks for that
  event, and someone with a task on it cannot register. The "Allocate task to" list offers
  members and volunteers who are not attending.
- **Checking email.** `make email-test TO=you@example.com` sends one sample ticket and says why
  if it could not. The ticket page states whether the email was sent, saved (no mail server) or failed.
- An event uses public registration or FairSeat ticket categories, never both.

Not built: a waitlist, member prices for guests, cancelling by the participant, refunding
everyone automatically when an event is cancelled (remove participants one by one to refund).

Tests: `make test-registrations`.

# Memberships, Tickets, Check-in, Announcements, Certificates, Handover

Six tools that used to be listed as "coming soon" are built into the FastAPI app.

| Tool | Page | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| MemberCore | `/memberships` | Plans, joining and renewing, dues (online, in person, or waived), the member discount on tickets |
| FairSeat | `/tickets` | Book seats at the server's price; pay; held seats waiting for payment |
| GateSync | `/tickets/mine`, `/check-in` | A QR code per ticket; scanning and a guest list at the door; one admission per ticket |
| CrewDoo Connect | `/announcements` | Announcements by audience (everyone, members, volunteers, officers, one event), read tracking |
| CertiDoo | `/certificates`, `/verify` | Certificates from attendance or finished tasks, a public check by code, revocation |
| LegacyVault | `/handover` | Handover notes per role, with every saved version kept |

## Switching them on (local database only)

Their tables are in `migrations/pending/` and `make migrate` does not apply them. A tool whose
tables are missing answers 404 and stays listed as not here yet, so nothing changes for a
database that has not been migrated.

```
make seed-all        # wipe the local database, load demo data, add the modules and their demo data
# or, keeping your current local data:
make modules         # add the tables and demo plans, tickets, announcements and notes
```

Then in `.env`: `FAIRSEAT_ENABLED=1` and `FAIRSEAT_PAYMENTS_ENABLED=1` (Tickets and Check-in
need both the tables and the switch). Restart `make run`.

`scripts/migrate_pending.py` refuses any database that is not on this machine. Applying these
migrations to the shared Supabase database is a team decision and has not been made.

## How the parts connect

- **Membership to ticket price.** `membership_service.MemberCoreMemberships` is FairSeat's
  membership source. An active, unexpired membership of the event's club gives the discount
  its term was bought with. A pending membership gives nothing. `users.is_active_member` is
  never the pricing authority; MemberCore keeps that flag in step for merchandise pricing.
- **Dues to the ledger.** Dues paid online or recorded in person post one income entry
  (`source_module = membership`). A waiver posts nothing.
- **Booking to QR code.** A confirmed booking's seats get a credential at once. The code is
  `whodoo.ticket.v1.` plus 32 bytes derived from the ticket id under `TICKET_CREDENTIAL_SECRET`;
  only its SHA-256 digest is stored. The format and digest match `web/src/modules/gatesync`.
- **Check-in to certificates.** Participation certificates go to people GateSync checked in;
  volunteering certificates to people who finished a CrewPulse task on the event.

## Who can do what

| Action | Roles |
|---|---|
| Create or edit plans, record a cash payment, waive dues, cancel a membership | Treasurer, president |
| Scan and check in | Volunteer, event lead, treasurer, president |
| Post an announcement | Event lead, treasurer, president (remove: the author or the president) |
| Issue or revoke a certificate | Treasurer, president; an event lead for an event they lead |
| Write a handover note | President (any role); treasurer and event leads (their own role, volunteers, everyone) |

## Not built

- Refunding membership dues on request, and partial refunds. A payment that arrives for dues
  already settled another way is refunded automatically.
- Camera scanning needs a browser with `BarcodeDetector` (Chrome, Edge, Android). Elsewhere the
  door page takes a typed or pasted code, a handheld scanner, or a tap on the guest list.
- Announcements are read in the app; nothing is emailed or pushed.
- The Next.js app in `web/` still has its own scanner and booking pages and is not yet pointed
  at these endpoints.
- Stripe has only been exercised through the mock provider for dues, as for tickets.
