✨ Meet WhoDoo

Student organizations juggle events, members, volunteers, tasks, and budgets. WhoDoo brings these workflows together in one place, helping teams spend less time coordinating and more time creating memorable experiences.

🚀 What can you do?

<table> <tr> <td align="center" width="33%"> <h3>📅</h3> <strong>Event Management</strong> <p>Plan events and keep everything organized.</p> </td> <td align="center" width="33%"> <h3>👥</h3> <strong>Member Management</strong> <p>Keep your community connected and engaged.</p> </td> <td align="center" width="33%"> <h3>✅</h3> <strong>Task Coordination</strong> <p>Make responsibilities clear and track progress.</p> </td> </tr> <tr> <td align="center"> <h3>🎟️</h3> <strong>Ticketing</strong> <p>Simplify event registration and attendance workflows.</p> </td> <td align="center"> <h3>🤝</h3> <strong>Volunteers</strong> <p>Coordinate teams and organize contributions.</p> </td> <td align="center"> <h3>💰</h3> <strong>Finances</strong> <p>Keep organizational budgets and expenses in view.</p> </td> </tr> </table>
